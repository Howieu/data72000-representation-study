\chapter{Literature Review}

\section{Representation in customer segmentation}

An event log records timestamped actions, such as viewing an item, adding it to a cart or purchasing it. Feature construction determines which similarities a clustering algorithm can observe. The choice would be neutral only if it left the relevant grouping unchanged.

Clickstream research derives behavioural segments from constraints on large, noisy action sequences \citep{dextras2019segmentifier}. Object-centred event-log research constructs vector profiles for clustering and pattern analysis \citep{faria2023eventlogs}, while customer-behaviour studies select features for particular decision tasks \citep{abdi2019cbmf}. These application-specific choices motivate examining representation within the model rather than assuming one universally appropriate customer description.

\section{Cluster structure and validation}

Internal validation measures feature geometry; external validation compares assignments with supplied labels; repeated fitting tests recurrence under specified input changes. These are distinct forms of evidence. Clusterability theory supplies incompatible notions of recoverable structure \citep{ackerman2009clusterability}, and empirical measures depend on distribution, dimension and the structure sought \citep{adolfsson2019clusterability}. A high internal score therefore supports a partition within a given space, not representation-independent customer types. Dimension reduction also changes geometry and may weaken the connection to named attributes \citep{bertsimas2021icot}.

\section{Explainable clustering}

Three explanation designs have different targets. Intrinsic methods expose the computation producing their own partition: CLASSIX records sorted aggregation groups, starting points and final assignments \citep{chen2024classix}. Explainability-first methods incorporate a simple description into cluster formation: ICOT optimises tree partitions, while XClusters balances distortion against tree size and accuracy \citep{bertsimas2021icot,hwang2023xclusters}. Post hoc methods approximate a reference partition with rules. Changing an explanation can consequently change the clustering in the second design, while the third evaluates an already fitted reference.

\section{Explanation complexity and fidelity}

IMM constructs threshold trees and bounds the additional K Means or K Medians cost imposed by that restriction \citep{moshkovitz2020imm}. Such simplicity can incur unavoidable objective loss depending on cluster count and dimension \citep{laber2021price}. A distance-cost guarantee is not a guarantee of label agreement. Methods that remove outliers before explaining a clustering also change the population being explained \citep{bandyapadhyay2022explanation}.

CREAM constructs rectangular regions through tree induction, using clustering during construction and permitting model-output approximation in supervised settings. Maximum depth and predictive-error thresholds control splitting \citep{sabbatini2023cream}. These controls differ from CLASSIX radius and group counts, or the distortion/tree tradeoffs of ICOT and XClusters. None directly measures human comprehension.

ExKMC expands a tree beyond one leaf per K Means cluster. Its cost analysis motivates varying the leaf budget but does not guarantee held-out label agreement; the source used here is the authors' arXiv preprint \citep{frost2020exkmc}. Here \emph{fidelity} means exact agreement between tree predictions and held-out K Means assignments. It measures approximation, not whether the reference clustering is correct. XAI research likewise shows why fidelity requires an explicit target and calculation \citep{mironicolau2025fidelity}.

\section{Research gap and positioning}

Operational usefulness is another evidential boundary. Named rules may be queryable and provenance may identify representatives, without establishing effective interventions or improved revenue. Segmentifier supports behavioural refinement and export, but does not validate clustering's commercial effect \citep{dextras2019segmentifier}. Objective-cost guarantees similarly do not establish adequate segment size or feature coverage.

The reviewed methods provide useful constructions and guarantees but do not establish that adding behavioural features improves all these properties together. This dissertation examines the link between representation, partition change, CLASSIX provenance, ExKMC fidelity and auditable addressability for the same purchasers. These remain separate assessments rather than components of one explainability score.

\chapter{Conclusion}

For the same 11,719 RetailRocket purchasers, compact and rich features produce substantially different K Means and CLASSIX assignments, including under matched parameters. High within-space resampling agreement therefore coexists with disagreement between representations.

Explanation properties also change. Rich ExKMC fidelity begins higher at four leaves, but compact exceeds it at sixteen. Rich CLASSIX has more aggregation groups, describing provenance detail rather than human difficulty. Only one rich K Means cluster passes the operational audit; accurate approximation and repeatable clustering alone do not establish addressability.

Sensitivity checks qualify these findings under feature deletion, dimension reduction, numerical perturbation and session changes. Projection analysis explains a valid computational shortcut; the local certificate supplies conditional distance-merging guarantees and does not certify the selected rich density-merging fit.

Representation is part of the empirical clustering model. Here richer behaviour changes assignments and explanation properties without consistently improving geometric quality or producing deployment-ready groups. Objectively correct customer types, human comprehension and realised business value remain unestablished.

## Notes

1. **No discrepancy was found** between any checked manuscript value and its source artefact. This means the numerical-freeze constraint (task specification §2) can be honoured without any correction to reported figures — the revision only needs to change surrounding prose (definitions, motivation, structure), never the numbers themselves.
2. **Word count** is the one "value" in the manuscript that is expected to change mechanically as a result of this revision (abstract shortened per SF-02; §3.2.3/§3.2.4 lengthened per SF-14 and the task's Chapter 3 requirements; terminology glosses added throughout). This is declared explicitly in `\FinalWordCount` and must be recomputed and updated after the prose edits are finalised, before final compilation. This is a mechanical correction to a self-referential document field, not a change to any reported empirical result, and is therefore outside the scope of the results freeze in task specification §2.
3. Coverage: every quantity on the task specification's "at minimum verify" list was checked except tree depth/leaves at intermediate leaf counts (5–15), which were sampled rather than checked exhaustively — spot checks at k'=5, 8, 12 in `exkmc_oos_fidelity.csv` also matched the monotonic pattern described in the text (fidelity rising, depth non-decreasing) with no anomalies.
